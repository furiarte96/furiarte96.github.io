Printing

  1. Tap Save under a sheet, then Save Image. Or press and hold the sheet and choose Save to Photos.
  2. Open it in Photos and tap Share, then Print.
  3. Pick 4×6 paper. Turn off any "fit" or "fill" scaling so each photo comes out exactly 2×3 inches.
Adjust photo

Drag to move. Pinch or use the slider to zoom.
