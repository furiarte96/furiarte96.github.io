Printing

  1. Tap Save under a sheet, then Save Image. Or press and hold the sheet and choose Save to Photos.
  2. Open it in Photos and tap Share, then Print.
  3. Pick 4×6 paper. Turn off any "fit" or "fill" scaling so each photo comes out at the exact size you picked.
